Established a new way to teach piano by leveraging AI, providing real-time musical technique and audio feedback. Implemented pose detection algorithms with MediaPipe and OpenCV to track hand position and proper wrist angle. Featured by McMaster University's CTO and the Faculty of Engineering's site for innovative application of AI in music education.
